EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
A final rule from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, Office of the Secretary, was published on 2026-09-29, correcting technical and typographical errors in a prior final rule concerning Medicare Program matters. This prior rule, published on August 4, 2026, addressed Hospital Inpatient Prospective Payment Systems for Acute Care Hospitals (IPPS) and the Long-Term Care Hospital Prospective Payment System, alongside policy changes and Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 Rates, Quality Programs requirements, other policy changes, and updated Health Information Technology Standards. The remaining activity included routine paperwork items.

----------------------------
A final rule from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, Office of the Secretary, published on 2026-09-29, corrects technical and typographical errors in a previous final rule.
