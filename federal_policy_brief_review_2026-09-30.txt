FEDERAL POLICY BRIEF
Coverage: 2026-09-23 to 2026-09-30
================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
A final rule from CMS regarding Medicare Program matters, including Hospital Inpatient Prospective Payment Systems for Acute Care Hospitals (IPPS) and the Long-Term Care Hospital Prospective Payment System, was published on 2026-09-29. The Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service issued a proposed rule on 2026-09-29 concerning the election to pay tax on gain from certain farmland property in four equal annual installments. Additionally, the Agriculture Department, Agricultural Marketing Service published a final rule on 2026-09-29 extending the inedible disposition obligation deadline for almonds grown in California. Other activities included a final rule from the CMS Office of the Secretary correcting technical and typographical errors in a prior final rule.

CMS (MEDICAID/CHIP/MEDICARE)
----------------------------
A final rule from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, Office of the Secretary, published on 2026-09-29, corrects technical and typographical errors in a prior final rule. This prior rule, published on August 4, 2026, addressed Medicare Program matters, specifically concerning Hospital Inpatient Prospective Payment Systems for Acute Care Hospitals (IPPS) and the Long-Term Care Hospital Prospective Payment System, as well as policy changes and Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 Rates, requirements for Quality Programs, other policy changes, and the adoption of updated versions of certain Health Information Technology Standards.

CROSS-PROGRAM
-------------
A proposed rule from the Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service, published 2026-09-29, addresses the statutory election to pay tax on gain from certain farmland property in four equal annual installments. This proposed regulation would affect taxpayers selling or exchanging qualified farmland to a qualified farmer who elects this payment structure.

Separately, a final rule from the Agriculture Department, Agricultural Marketing Service, published 2026-09-29, extends the inedible disposition obligation deadline for almonds grown in California. This extension, which implements a recommendation from the Almond Board of California, moves the deadline from September 30 to November 30 indefinitely.
