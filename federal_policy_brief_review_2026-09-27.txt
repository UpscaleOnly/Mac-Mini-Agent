FEDERAL POLICY BRIEF
Coverage: 2026-09-20 to 2026-09-27
================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
CMS issued a Privacy Act matching program notice on 2026-09-25 regarding the re-establishment of a matching program with the U.S. Department of the Treasury, Bureau of Fiscal Service, alongside an information collection request for Medicaid and CHIP and a proposed rule for Medicare Part D. For other state programs, the Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration, published two information collection requests on 2026-09-24 concerning program data collection for the Turnip the Beet High Quality Summer Meals Program and FNA Quick Response Surveys. The Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, issued a Privacy Act system of records notice on 2026-09-28 and a notice on 2026-09-24 regarding a cooperative agreement with the International Rescue Committee. Other significant updates include the FDA's proposed rule on 2026-09-22 substituting "animal" with "nonclinical" and a final rule on 2026-09-25 permitting vitamin D2 inactive bakers yeast, while the Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service, issued a final rule on 2026-09-25 increasing the estate tax user fee, among other rules and notices from various federal agencies.

CMS (MEDICAID/CHIP/MEDICARE)
----------------------------
CMS issued a Privacy Act matching program notice on 2026-09-25 regarding the re-establishment of a matching program between CMS and the Do Not Pay Working System, which is administered by the U.S. Department of the Treasury, Bureau of Fiscal Service.

For Medicaid and CHIP, CMS published an information collection request on 2026-09-25. This notice seeks public comment on generic information collection activities, which fall within the scope of Medicaid and CHIP state plan amendments, waivers, demonstrations, and reporting. Interested parties are invited to submit comments regarding burden estimates, the necessity and utility of the proposed collection, and ways to use automated collection techniques.

Separately, CMS published a proposed rule on 2026-09-24 that is a Request for Information concerning Medicare Part D. This RFI solicits input for establishing standards for reasonable and relevant pharmacy contract terms and conditions. These standards are required by the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2026, for plan years beginning January 1, 2029.

SNAP
----
An information collection request issued by the Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration, was published on 2026-09-24 regarding program data collection. This notice invites comment on a proposed collection for the Turnip the Beet High Quality Summer Meals Program. This collection is a revision of an approved collection designed to recognize program sponsors' nutrition efforts within the Summer Food Service Program (SFSP) or the National School Lunch Program (NSLP) Seamless Summer Option (SSO).

Additionally, the Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration, published a second information collection request on 2026-09-24 concerning a proposed collection for Generic Clearance for FNA Quick Response Surveys (FNA QRS). This notice invites comment on a revision to an approved collection used to conduct short, quick-turnaround surveys of State and local agencies. The surveys cover services provided in multiple programs, including the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children (WIC), National School Lunch Program (NSLP), School Breakfast Program (SBP), Special Milk Program (SMP), Fresh Fruit and Vegetable Program (FFVP), Summer Food Service Program (SFSP), Summer EBT (SEBT), Team Nutrition, Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP), Food Distribution Program on Indian Reservations (FDPIR), The Emergency Food Assistance Program (TEFAP), and the Commodity Supplemental Food Program (CSFP).

TANF
----
A Privacy Act system of records notice issued by the Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, published on 2026-09-28, details modifications to an existing system of records maintained by the Office of Refugee Resettlement (ORR). This system, which covers records for unaccompanied children, their intended sponsors, and household members, is changing its name to "ORR Unaccompanied Alien Children Bureau (UACB) Administrative Program Records." The modification involves streamlining certain routine uses to enhance information sharing for child protection purposes while maintaining core privacy protections.

Separately, a notice issued by the Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, published on 2026-09-24, announces the intent to award a single-source cooperative agreement up to $20,000,000 to the International Rescue Committee in New York, New York. This agreement, for the National Virtual Program for Professional Advancement, has a proposed project period from September 30, 2026, through September 29, 2027. The initiative will use a nationwide virtual service delivery model to provide professional advancement services to up to 1,334 eligible refugees who arrived in the United States between September 1, 2026, and September 30, 2026.

CROSS-PROGRAM
-------------
The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) issued two related actions regarding scientific terminology. A proposed rule issued by the FDA on 2026-09-22 proposes substituting references to "animal" tests or studies with "nonclinical" tests or studies, among other changes. Separately, the FDA issued a direct final rule on 2026-09-22 making similar amendments, which substitutes "animal" with "nonclinical" and adds definitions for "nonclinical test" and "nonclinical study." In other health-related updates, the FDA issued a final rule on 2026-09-25 amending food additive regulations to permit the safe use of vitamin D2 inactive bakers yeast. The FDA also published a proposed rule on 2026-09-25 announcing a color additive petition from Doehler GmbH regarding calcium sulfate.

Several documents address agricultural and trade regulations. The Agriculture Department, Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service, published a proposed rule on 2026-09-24 proposing to reduce the timeframe for highly pathogenic avian influenza (HPAI) import requirements from 90 days to 28 days. The Agriculture Department, Office of the Secretary, published a proposed rule on 2026-09-25 extending the public comment period by 20 days on an RFI related to 9 CFR parts 101-118, 123-124. Additionally, the Agriculture Department, Agricultural Marketing Service, issued a final rule on 2026-09-22 clarifying the assessment rate for softwood lumber and revising the membership of the Softwood Lumber Board.

Financial and state exchange regulations were also updated. The Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service, issued a final rule on 2026-09-25 increasing the user fee for requesting an estate tax closing letter from $56 to $76. Furthermore, the Health and Human Services Department issued a final rule on 2026-09-23 establishing a temporary moratorium on agent and broker registration for the Federally-facilitated Exchanges for Plan Year 2027. Finally, the Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service, published a proposed rule on 2026-09-28 concerning information reporting for Qualified Opportunity Zones and updated procedures for Qualified Opportunity Funds.
