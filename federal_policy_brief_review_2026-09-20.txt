FEDERAL POLICY BRIEF
Coverage: 2026-09-13 to 2026-09-20
================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
The Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, issued three notices, including one announcing an annual adjustment to the amount in controversy threshold amounts for the Medicare appeals process, and another announcing a public meeting for new revisions to the Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System Level II Coding. The Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration, published multiple notices regarding Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program demonstration projects for North Dakota, Virginia, Nevada, Wyoming, Missouri, Texas, Arkansas, Florida, Hawaii, Idaho, Kansas, Oklahoma, Montana, Utah, Indiana, Louisiana, South Carolina, and Ohio. Regarding data collection, the Administration for Children and Families published two information collection requests concerning the Diaper Distribution Demonstration and Research Pilot and the Multistate Financial Institution Data Match. Several major regulatory actions were finalized, including a final rule from the Food and Drug Administration concerning radiology and computer-aided diagnostic devices, and a final rule from the Social Security Administration regarding revised medical criteria for evaluating cardiovascular disorders. Additionally, the Agriculture Department, Agricultural Marketing Service, published a final rule amending the pricing provisions in the 11 Federal Milk Marketing Orders, while the Internal Revenue Service published a proposed rule providing a notice of public hearing regarding private school tax exemptions.

CMS (MEDICAID/CHIP/MEDICARE)
----------------------------
A notice issued by the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, published 2026-09-17, acknowledges the approval of an application from DNV Healthcare USA Inc. (DNV) for continued CMS recognition as a national accrediting organization for hospitals participating in the Medicare or Medicaid programs.

Regarding coding and billing, a notice published by the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, on 2026-09-16, announces a public meeting for new revisions to the Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) Level II Coding. This meeting will discuss CMS preliminary coding, Medicare benefit category, and Medicare payment determinations for non-drug and non-biological items and services.

Finally, a notice from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, published 2026-09-16, announces the annual adjustment to the amount in controversy (AIC) threshold amounts for the Medicare appeals process. These adjusted amounts are effective for requests filed on or after January 1, 2027, setting the threshold at $200 for Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) hearings and $2,000 for judicial review.

SNAP
----
On September 15, 2026, the Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration, published multiple notices regarding Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program demonstration projects. These notices invite the public to comment on demonstration projects for several states, including North Dakota, Virginia, Nevada, Wyoming, Missouri, Texas, Arkansas, Florida, Hawaii, Idaho, Kansas, Oklahoma, Montana, Utah, Indiana, Louisiana, South Carolina, and Ohio.

Each notice concerns a state's project authorized under section 17(b) of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008, 7 U.S.C. 2026(b). The abstract for these notices states that the Food and Nutrition Administration will take appropriate action as necessary in response to public comments received.

TANF
----
Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, published an (information collection request) on 2026-09-17 regarding the Diaper Distribution Demonstration and Research Pilot. The Office of Community Services (OCS), Administration for Children and Families (ACF), is proposing to continue data collection for this pilot (OMB #: 0970-0642). While no changes are proposed to the Beneficiary Report, OCS is proposing revisions to the Beneficiary Survey and adding a new Organizational Survey.

Separately, the same agency published an (information collection request) on 2026-09-15 concerning the Multistate Financial Institution Data Match (MSFIDM) with Federally Assisted State Transmitted (FAST) Levy. The Office of Child Support Enforcement (OCSE), Administration for Children and Families (ACF), is requesting a 3-year extension for this match (OMB #: 0970-0196, which expires January 31, 2027). OCSE revised the MSFIDM handbook and the FAST Levy record specifications, and burden estimates were reduced by 32.71 percent.

CROSS-PROGRAM
-------------
A final rule issued by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) on September 17, 2026, provides a final determination regarding a partial exemption from premarket notification requirements for specific radiology and computer-aided diagnostic and triage devices. This order follows the FDA's denial of a petition issued on April 1, 2026. Separately, the Social Security Administration published a final rule on September 16, 2026, concerning revised medical criteria for evaluating cardiovascular disorders.

In other policy areas, the Agriculture Department, Agricultural Marketing Service, published a final rule on September 18, 2026, amending the pricing provisions in the 11 Federal Milk Marketing Orders. This rule corrects the Class I differential in Morgan County, Ohio, and removes Shannon County, South Dakota, from the Class I Differential table.

Finally, the Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service, published a proposed rule on September 16, 2026, providing a notice of public hearing. These proposed regulations would update existing rules to state that a private school is not exempt from Federal income tax if it discriminates on the basis of race, color, or national or ethnic origin in its administration of educational, admissions, or scholarship policies.
