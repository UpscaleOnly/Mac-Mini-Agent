FEDERAL POLICY BRIEF
Coverage: 2026-09-13 to 2026-09-20
================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
The Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, issued three notices regarding HCPCS Level II Coding, Medicare appeals adjustments, and DNV accreditation. The Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration published multiple notices concerning Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program demonstration projects for states including North Dakota, Virginia, Nevada, Wyoming, Missouri, Texas, Arkansas, Florida, Hawaii, Idaho, Kansas, Oklahoma, Montana, Utah, Indiana, Louisiana, South Carolina, and Ohio. The Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration published two information collection requests regarding the Diaper Distribution Demonstration and Research Pilot and the Multistate Financial Institution Data Match. Key regulatory actions include a final rule from the Food and Drug Administration on medical devices, a final rule from the Social Security Administration on cardiovascular criteria, and a proposed rule from the Internal Revenue Service on racial nondiscrimination in private schools. Other updates include a final rule from the Agriculture Department, Agricultural Marketing Service, amending milk pricing provisions, alongside various other notices and requests.

CMS (MEDICAID/CHIP/MEDICARE)
----------------------------
A notice issued by the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, on September 16, 2026, announces a public meeting for new revisions to the Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) Level II Coding. This meeting will discuss preliminary coding, Medicare benefit category, and Medicare payment determinations for non-drug and non-biological items and services.

Separately, a notice from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, also published on September 16, 2026, announces the annual adjustment to the Amount in Controversy threshold amounts for the Medicare appeals process. These adjusted amounts are effective for requests filed on or after January 1, 2027, setting the threshold at $200 for Administrative Law Judge hearings and $2,000 for judicial review.

Finally, a notice published by the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, on September 17, 2026, acknowledges the approval of an application from DNV Healthcare USA Inc. This notice confirms continued CMS recognition of DNV as a national accrediting organization for hospitals participating in the Medicare or Medicaid programs.

SNAP
----
Multiple (notice) documents were published on 2026-09-15 by the Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration, concerning Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) demonstration projects. These notices invite the public to comment on demonstration projects for several states, including North Dakota, Virginia, Nevada, Wyoming, Missouri, Texas, Arkansas, Florida, Hawaii, Idaho, Kansas, Oklahoma, Montana, Utah, Indiana, Louisiana, South Carolina, and Ohio.

Each notice specifies that the demonstration project is authorized under section 17(b) of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008, 7 U.S.C. 2026(b). The Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration states that it will take appropriate action as necessary in response to public comments received.

TANF
----
Two information collection requests were published by the Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration. One request, published 2026-09-17, concerns the Diaper Distribution Demonstration and Research Pilot (DDDRP). The Office of Community Services (OCS), Administration for Children and Families, is proposing to continue collecting data for this pilot (OMB #: 0970-0642). This collection includes the Beneficiary Report, which is not proposed for change, but OCS is proposing revisions to the Beneficiary Survey and adding a new Organizational Survey.

A separate information collection request, published 2026-09-15, addresses the Multistate Financial Institution Data Match (MSFIDM) with Federally Assisted State Transmitted (FAST) Levy. The Office of Child Support Enforcement (OCSE), Administration for Children and Families, is requesting a 3-year extension for this data match (OMB #: 0970-0196), which currently expires January 31, 2027. OCSE revised the MSFIDM handbook and the FAST Levy record specifications, and burden estimates were reduced by 32.71 percent.

CROSS-PROGRAM
-------------
The Food and Drug Administration published a final rule concerning medical devices, setting forth its final determination regarding a partial exemption from premarket notification requirements for radiology computer-aided detection and/or diagnosis devices and computer-aided triage and notification devices. This order follows FDA's denial of a petition, which had requested exemption for specific generic device types. Separately, the Social Security Administration issued a final rule regarding revised medical criteria for evaluating cardiovascular disorders.

Other regulatory updates include a final rule from the Agriculture Department, Agricultural Marketing Service, which amends the pricing provisions in the 11 Federal Milk Marketing Orders. This amendment corrects the Class I differential in Morgan County, Ohio, and removes Shannon County, South Dakota, from the Class I Differential table.

Finally, the Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service, published a notice of public hearing regarding proposed regulations on racial nondiscrimination in private schools. These proposed regulations would update existing rules to state that a private school is not exempt from Federal income tax if it discriminates on the basis of race, color, or national or ethnic origin in its policies.
