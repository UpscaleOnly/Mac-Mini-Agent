Federal Policy Brief

Coverage: 2026-09-24 to 2026-10-01

Executive Summary

A proposed rule from Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services regarding Medicare Part D Reasonable and Relevant Pharmacy Contracting Standards was published on 2026-09-24, alongside two information collection requests from the Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration concerning SNAP revisions. The Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration issued a notice on 2026-09-28 detailing a modification to a Privacy Act system of records for unaccompanied children. Additionally, the Food and Drug Administration issued a final rule amending food additive regulations to permit the use of Vitamin D2 Inactive Bakers Yeast. Other actions included an information collection request from Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, a Privacy Act matching program notice between Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services and the U.S. Department of the Treasury, and several final and proposed rules from the Health and Human Services Department and Food and Drug Administration.

CMS (Medicaid/CHIP/Medicare)

An information collection request from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, was published on 2026-09-25. This request seeks public comment on information collections related to Medicaid and Children's Health Insurance Program (CHIP) that fall under an existing generic clearance process. Interested persons may submit comments regarding burden estimates, the necessity and utility of the proposed information collection, and methods to enhance the quality or minimize the burden of the information collected.

Separately, a proposed rule from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, was published on 2026-09-24. This request for information solicits input regarding standards for reasonable and relevant pharmacy contract terms and conditions under the Medicare prescription drug benefit. This action is required by Section 6223(a) of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2026 (CAA, 2026), which mandates that Part D plan sponsors permit pharmacies meeting these standards to participate in the plan network for plan years beginning January 1, 2029.

Sources:

  • Medicaid and Children's Health Insurance Program (CHIP) Generic Information Collection Activities: Proposed Collection; Comment Request (information collection request, 2026-09-25)
  • Request for Information; Medicare Part D Reasonable and Relevant Pharmacy Contracting Standards (proposed rule, 2026-09-24)

SNAP

An information collection request from the Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration, published 2026-09-24, invites public and agency comment on a revision to an approved collection. This collection relates to recognizing program sponsors' nutrition efforts within the Summer Food Service Program (SFSP) or the National School Lunch Program (NSLP) Seamless Summer Option (SSO).

Additionally, the Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration, published another information collection request on 2026-09-24. This notice seeks comment on a revision to an approved collection used to conduct short, quick-turnaround surveys of State and local agencies. These surveys cover the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), Child Nutrition Programs, and Supplemental Nutrition and Safety Programs administered federally by the Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA). The programs listed include SNAP, WIC, NSLP, SBP, SMP, FFVP, SFSP, SEBT, Team Nutrition, the Patrick Leahy Farm to School Program, CACFP, USDA Foods in Schools, FDPIR, TEFAP, and CSFP.

  • Agency Information Collection Activities: Proposed Collection; Comment Request-Generic Clearance for FNA Quick Response Surveys (FNA QRS) (information collection request, 2026-09-24)

TANF

A Privacy Act system of records notice from the Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, was published on 2026-09-28. This notice details a modification to System No. 09-80-0321, maintained by the Office of Refugee Resettlement within the Administration for Children and Families. The system covers records for unaccompanied children, their intended sponsors, and household members, used in administering ORR programs. The modification changes the system of records name to "ORR Unaccompanied Alien Children Bureau (UACB) Administrative Program Records" and streamlines routine uses to enhance information sharing for child protection while keeping core privacy protections.

Additionally, a notice from the Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, was published on 2026-09-24. This notice announces the intent to award a single-source cooperative agreement up to $20,000,000 to the International Rescue Committee in New York, New York. This agreement is for the National Virtual Program for Professional Advancement, with a proposed project period from September 30, 2026, through September 29, 2027. The initiative intends to provide professional advancement services to up to 1,334 eligible refugees who arrived in the United States between September 1, 2026, and September 30, 2026.

Sources:

  • Privacy Act of 1974; System of Records (Privacy Act system of records notice, 2026-09-28)
  • Announcement of the Intent To Award a Single-Source Cooperative Agreement to International Rescue Committee in New York, New York (notice, 2026-09-24)

IT Governance

A Privacy Act matching program notice from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, was published on 2026-09-25. This notice concerns the re-establishment of a matching program between CMS and the Do Not Pay Working System, which is administered by the U.S. Department of the Treasury, Bureau of Fiscal Service (Fiscal Service). This action is taken in accordance with subsection (e)(12) of the Privacy Act of 1974, as amended.

Sources:

Cross-Program

A final rule from the Food and Drug Administration, issued by the Health and Human Services Department, amends food additive regulations to allow the safe use of vitamin D2 inactive bakers yeast as a vitamin D2 source in specific food categories, following a petition from Lallemand Inc.

The Food and Drug Administration also issued a direct final rule, under the Health and Human Services Department, changing terminology in its regulations. This rule substitutes references to "animal" tests or studies with "nonclinical" tests or studies, adds definitions for "nonclinical test" and "nonclinical study," and substitutes "nonclinical" for "preclinical" and "in vitro" for consistency.

Separately, the Health and Human Services Department issued an interim final rule with comment period, under the Health and Human Services Department, establishing a temporary moratorium on the registration of agents and brokers who lack a current Plan Year registration with the Federally-facilitated Exchanges when seeking to enter into Exchange agreements with the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services for Plan Year 2027. This moratorium is set to end on February 1, 2027.

Additionally, the Food and Drug Administration announced a proposed rule, under the Health and Human Services Department, regarding a color additive petition from Doehler GmbH, proposing an amendment to color additive regulations to allow the safe use of calcium sulfate in various foods at levels consistent with good manufacturing practice.
