Federal Policy Brief

Coverage: 2026-09-24 to 2026-10-01

Executive Summary

A proposed rule from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, regarding Medicare Part D Reasonable and Relevant Pharmacy Contracting Standards was published, alongside an information collection request from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, concerning Medicaid and CHIP Generic Information Collection Activities. The Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration issued two information collection requests regarding revisions to collections for the Summer Food Service Program and for conducting quick-turnaround surveys for multiple nutrition programs. Other actions included a Privacy Act system of records notice from the Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, and a Privacy Act matching program notice from Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services. The remaining activity included a final rule from the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) and Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) on a temporary moratorium on agent and broker registrations, alongside other final and proposed rules from the Food and Drug Administration.

CMS (Medicaid/CHIP/Medicare)

A notice from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, published 2026-09-25, is an information collection request regarding Medicaid and Children's Health Insurance Program (CHIP) Generic Information Collection Activities. This notice seeks public comment on proposed collections of information that CMS believes are generic and fall within an existing umbrella scope that covers Medicaid and CHIP State plan amendments, waivers, demonstrations, and reporting. Interested persons can submit comments on the burden estimates or other aspects of this collection of information.

Additionally, a proposed rule from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, published 2026-09-24, is a Request for Information concerning Medicare Part D Reasonable and Relevant Pharmacy Contracting Standards. This RFI is prompted by Section 6223(a) of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2026, which requires Part D plan sponsors to permit pharmacies meeting standard contract terms to participate as a network pharmacy. The Act also requires that, for plan years beginning January 1, 2029, these standard contract terms and conditions must be reasonable and relevant according to standards established by the Secretary of the Department of Health and Human Services.

SNAP

An information collection request from the Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration, published 2026-09-24, invites comments on a proposed revision to an existing collection. This revision relates to recognizing program sponsors' nutrition efforts in the Summer Food Service Program (SFSP) or the National School Lunch Program (NSLP) Seamless Summer Option (SSO).

Additionally, the Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration, published another information collection request on 2026-09-24. This notice seeks public and agency comment on a proposed collection for conducting quick-turnaround surveys. These surveys cover State and local agencies providing services in the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), Child Nutrition Programs, and Supplemental Nutrition and Safety Programs administered by the Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA). The programs covered by this survey include SNAP, Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children (WIC), National School Lunch Program (NSLP), School Breakfast Program (SBP), Special Milk Program (SMP), Fresh Fruit and Vegetable Program (FFVP), Summer Food Service Program (SFSP), Summer EBT (SEBT), Team Nutrition, the Patrick Leahy Farm to School Program, the Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP), USDA Foods in Schools, Food Distribution Program on Indian Reservations (FDPIR), The Emergency Food Assistance Program (TEFAP), and the Commodity Supplemental Food Program (CSFP).

TANF

A Privacy Act system of records notice from the Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, was published on 2026-09-28. This notice details a modification to an existing system of records maintained by the Office of Refugee Resettlement (ORR) within HHS' Administration for Children and Families (ACF), System No. 09-80-0321, ORR Unaccompanied Children Bureau (UCB) Administrative Program Records. The records cover unaccompanied children, their intended sponsors, and members of the intended sponsors' households, used for administering ORR programs for care and placement. The modification changes the system of records name to "ORR Unaccompanied Alien Children Bureau (UACB) Administrative Program Records" and streamlines routine uses for child protection information sharing while keeping core privacy protections.

Additionally, a notice from the Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, was published on 2026-09-24. This notice announces the intent to award a single-source cooperative agreement up to $20,000,000 to International Rescue Committee in New York, New York. The agreement is for the National Virtual Program for Professional Advancement, with a proposed project period from September 30, 2026, through September 29, 2027. This initiative will allow IRC to use its virtual service delivery model to provide professional advancement services to up to 1,334 eligible refugees who arrived in the United States between September 1, 2026, and September 30, 2026.

Cross-Program

An interim final rule with comment period (IFC) from the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) and Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) details a temporary moratorium on agent and broker registrations for the Federally-facilitated Exchanges. This moratorium pauses registration for agents and brokers lacking Plan Year 2026 Exchange agreements while CMS implements enhanced program-integrity safeguards. The rule states that agents and brokers without Plan Year 2026 Exchange agreements will not be able to complete registration with the Federally-facilitated Exchanges for Plan Year 2027 until the moratorium ends on February 1, 2027.

Regarding food additive regulations, the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) issued a final rule amending regulations to allow for the safe use of vitamin D2 inactive bakers yeast as a source of vitamin D2 in specific food categories, following a petition from Lallemand Inc. Separately, the FDA published a proposed rule announcing a color additive petition from Doehler GmbH proposing to amend color additive regulations for the safe use of calcium sulfate in various foods at levels consistent with good manufacturing practice.

The FDA also issued a direct final rule and a proposed rule concerning nonclinical testing terminology. Both actions substitute references to "animal" tests or studies with "nonclinical" tests or studies, add definitions for "nonclinical test" and "nonclinical study," and substitute "nonclinical" for "preclinical" and "in vitro" for consistency. These changes align with amendments to the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and the Public Health Service Act and are intended to remove an emphasis on animal testing as the sole methodology for assessing drug safety in the nonclinical setting.
