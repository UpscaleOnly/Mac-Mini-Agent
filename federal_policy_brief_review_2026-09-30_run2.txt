FEDERAL POLICY BRIEF
Coverage: 2026-09-23 to 2026-09-30
================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
A final rule from CMS regarding the Medicare Program, Hospital Inpatient Prospective Payment Systems for Acute Care Hospitals (IPPS) and the Long-Term Care Hospital Prospective Payment System and Policy Changes and Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 Rates, was published on 2026-09-29. Additionally, the Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service issued a proposed rule on 2026-09-29 concerning the election to pay tax on gain from certain farmland property in four equal annual installments. The Agriculture Department, Agricultural Marketing Service published a final rule on 2026-09-29 extending the inedible disposition obligation deadline for almonds grown in California. Other activity included a final rule from the Agriculture Department, Agricultural Marketing Service.

CMS (MEDICAID/CHIP/MEDICARE)
----------------------------
A final rule from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, Office of the Secretary, published on 2026-09-29, corrects technical and typographical errors in a previous final rule. This corrected rule pertains to the Medicare Program and covers Hospital Inpatient Prospective Payment Systems for Acute Care Hospitals (IPPS) and the Long-Term Care Hospital Prospective Payment System and Policy Changes and Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 Rates, as well as requirements for quality programs, other policy changes, and the adoption of updated versions of certain health information technology standards.

CROSS-PROGRAM
-------------
A proposed rule from the Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service, published 2026-09-29, addresses the statutory election to pay tax on gain from certain farmland property in four equal annual installments. This proposed regulation would affect taxpayers selling or exchanging qualified farmland to a qualified farmer who elects this installment payment option.

Separately, a final rule from the Agriculture Department, Agricultural Marketing Service, published 2026-09-29, extends the inedible disposition obligation deadline for almonds grown in California. This rule implements a recommendation from the Almond Board of California, moving the deadline from September 30 to November 30 indefinitely under the Federal marketing order.
