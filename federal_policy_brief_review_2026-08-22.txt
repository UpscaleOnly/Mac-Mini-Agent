FEDERAL POLICY BRIEF
Coverage: 2026-08-15 to 2026-08-22
================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
HHS issued a Privacy Act system of records notice from Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services regarding the Nurses for Nursing Homes Program (NNHP), and HHS also issued a Privacy Act system of records notice from Children and Families Administration delaying the effective date of the modified system of records for TANF Data. The Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration, published an information collection request regarding the Understanding Participant Experiences in Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) Employment and Training (E&T) study and a proposed rule extending the comment period for Changes in Federal-State Administrative Cost Sharing. Regulatory updates included the FDA issuing a final rule reclassifying ISH test systems and a proposed rule reclassifying diagnostic endoscopic light source systems, while the IRS issued a final rule concerning general income taxes and a proposed rule addressing PRWORA application to refundable credits. Other actions included a Privacy Act matching program notice from the Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service, and various other proposed rules and final rules from multiple agencies.

CMS (MEDICAID/CHIP/MEDICARE)
----------------------------
A Privacy Act system of records notice issued by the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, was published on 2026-08-20. This notice announces the establishment of a new system of records, titled "Nurses for Nursing Homes Program (NNHP)," under system No. 09-70-0545.

The new system of records, which falls under the Privacy Act of 1974, will collect records on individuals who apply for, participate in, or otherwise support the NNHP. The records maintained will include personally identifiable information such as demographic data, professional licensure and credential information, education and training details, employment and nursing facility affiliation, payment information, tax reporting, and audit records.

CMS plans to use these records to support a nursing workforce incentive program, which is designed to strengthen the workforce serving Medicare and Medicaid-certified nursing homes, with a specific focus on underserved and rural communities. The scope of this new system of records is commensurate with its purpose of supporting the Nursing Home Staffing Campaign/Nursing Incentive Program (NHSC/NIP).

SNAP
----
The Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration, published an information collection request on 2026-08-21 regarding the Understanding Participant Experiences in Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) Employment and Training (E&T) study. This notice invites comments on a proposed information collection intended to gather data on how SNAP participants experience SNAP work requirements and the SNAP E&T program. The goal of this collection is to identify recommendations that can help programs improve customer service and efficiently connect participants with necessary training and services.

Additionally, the Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration, issued a proposed rule on 2026-08-17 concerning Changes in Federal-State Administrative Cost Sharing for SNAP. This action extends the public comment period for the rule, which was originally published on June 24, 2026. The public comment period is extended from August 24, 2026, to September 8, 2026.

TANF
----
A Privacy Act system of records notice issued by the Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, published 2026-08-20, states that the Department of Health and Human Services is delaying the effective date of the new routine use under the modified system of records maintained by the Office of Family Assistance (OFA) within HHS' Administration for Children and Families (ACF), System No. 09-80-0375, Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) Data.

Regarding administrative reporting, a notice issued by the Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, published 2026-08-18, indicates that ACF is requesting to reinstate approval of the ACF-700: Tribal Annual Report (OMB #: 0970-0430) with proposed revisions, specifically requesting changes to the form that reduce annual burden hours.

Separately, a notice issued by the Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, published 2026-08-18, announces one Tribal Consultation session between HHS/ACF OHS leadership and Tribal governments operating Head Start and Early Head Start programs. This consultation aims to discuss ways to better meet the needs of American Indian and Alaska Native (AIAN) children and their families, considering funding allocations, distribution formulas, and other issues affecting Head Start services.

Finally, a notice issued by the Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, published 2026-08-17, reports that ACF, Office of Refugee Resettlement (ORR), is withdrawing the Announcement of the Intent to Award a Single-Source Cooperative Agreement to Burke Law Group, PLLC in Houston, Texas, which was published on August 6, 2026, at 91 FR 50848 (FR Doc. 2026-16081).

CROSS-PROGRAM
-------------
The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) issued several updates regarding medical devices and food additives. A final rule published on 2026-08-17 reclassified in situ hybridization (ISH) test systems for use with certain oncology therapeutic products, changing them from postamendments class III to class II, subject to premarket notification. Additionally, the FDA issued a proposed rule on 2026-08-17 proposing to reclassify diagnostic endoscopic light source systems, which are postamendments class III devices, into class II, subject to premarket notification. The FDA also published a proposed rule on 2026-08-17 seeking public comment on classifying certain accessories into class I, distinct from other devices.

Regarding food additives, the FDA published a final rule on 2026-08-19 amending regulations to allow chromium DL-methionine chelate as a nutritional source of chromium in cattle feed. Several proposed rules were also issued on 2026-08-19, including one concerning the safe use of safflower extract as a color additive, another proposing the safe use of acetone as a solvent in carrot oil, and a third proposing to expand the safe use of gardenia blue in various foods.

In the area of tax and finance, the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) issued a final rule on 2026-08-21 concerning general income taxes. A proposed rule from the Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service, published on 2026-08-20, addresses the application of the Personal Responsibility and Work Opportunity Reconciliation Act of 1996 (PRWORA) to refundable federal income tax credits, stating that the refunded portion of certain credits would be considered a "Federal public benefit," potentially affecting eligibility for taxpayers who are not "qualified aliens." Furthermore, a proposed rule from the Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service, published on 2026-08-17, proposes removing a reporting requirement for certain trusts whose charitable contribution deductions come solely from passthrough entities.

The Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service, also issued a Privacy Act matching program notice on 2026-08-21 regarding the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) Disclosure of Information to Federal, State and Local Agencies (DIFSLA) Computer Matching Program. This notice stated that the Louisiana Department of Health is modifying its Computer Matching Agreement, and the Louisiana Department of Children and Families will no longer participate in the DIFSLA Program.
