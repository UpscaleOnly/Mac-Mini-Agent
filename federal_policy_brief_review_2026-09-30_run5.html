Executive Summary

A final rule from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, Office of the Secretary, was published on 2026-09-29, correcting technical and typographical errors related to the Medicare Program. This correction pertains to the final rule from August 4, 2026, which covered Hospital Inpatient Prospective Payment Systems for Acute Care Hospitals (IPPS) and the Long-Term Care Hospital Prospective Payment System and Policy Changes and Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 Rates, alongside quality program requirements and health information technology standards. The remaining activity included routine paperwork items.

CMS (Medicaid/CHIP/Medicare)

A final rule from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, Office of the Secretary, published on 2026-09-29, corrects technical and typographical errors. This correction pertains to the final rule that appeared in the Federal Register on August 4, 2026, concerning the Medicare Program. The subject matter of that rule included Hospital Inpatient Prospective Payment Systems for Acute Care Hospitals (IPPS) and the Long-Term Care Hospital Prospective Payment System and Policy Changes and Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 Rates, as well as requirements for quality programs, other policy changes, and the adoption of updated versions of certain health information technology standards.
