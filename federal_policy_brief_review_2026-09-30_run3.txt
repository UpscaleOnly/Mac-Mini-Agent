FEDERAL POLICY BRIEF
Coverage: 2026-09-23 to 2026-09-30
================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
A final rule from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, published on 2026-09-29, corrects technical and typographical errors regarding Medicare Program matters, including Hospital Inpatient Prospective Payment Systems for Acute Care Hospitals (IPPS) and the Long-Term Care Hospital Prospective Payment System and Policy Changes and Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 Rates. Additionally, the Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service, issued a proposed rule on 2026-09-29 concerning the election to pay tax on gain from certain farmland property in four equal annual installments. Other activity included routine paperwork items.

CMS (MEDICAID/CHIP/MEDICARE)
----------------------------
A final rule from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, Office of the Secretary, published on 2026-09-29, corrects technical and typographical errors in a prior final rule. This corrected rule addresses Medicare Program matters, specifically concerning Hospital Inpatient Prospective Payment Systems for Acute Care Hospitals (IPPS) and the Long-Term Care Hospital Prospective Payment System and Policy Changes and Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 Rates. It also covers requirements for quality programs, other policy changes, and the adoption of updated versions of certain health information technology standards.

CROSS-PROGRAM
-------------
A proposed rule from the Treasury Department, Internal Revenue Service, published on 2026-09-29, addresses the statutory election to pay tax on gain from certain farmland property in four equal annual installments. These proposed regulations would impact taxpayers who sell or exchange qualified farmland to a qualified farmer and choose to pay the resulting tax in equal annual installments over four years.
