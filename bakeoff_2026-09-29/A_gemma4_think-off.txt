FEDERAL POLICY BRIEF
Coverage: 2026-09-15 to 2026-09-29
================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
CMS issued notices regarding DNV Healthcare USA Inc.'s continued recognition, the second biannual HCPCS Level II public meeting of 2026, and annual adjustments to the amount in controversy threshold amounts for Medicare appeals. The Agriculture Department published notices concerning Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program demonstration projects for North Dakota, Virginia, Nevada, Wyoming, Missouri, Texas, Arkansas, Florida, Hawaii, Idaho, Kansas, Oklahoma, Montana, Utah, Indiana, Louisiana, South Carolina, and Ohio. The Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, issued information collection requests concerning the Diaper Distribution Demonstration and Research Pilot and the Multistate Financial Institution Data Match (MSFIDM) with Federally Assisted State Transmitted (FAST) Levy. Other actions included a final rule from the Agriculture Department's Agricultural Marketing Service amending pricing provisions in the 11 Federal Milk Marketing Orders (FMMOs), a final rule from the Food and Drug Administration regarding a partial exemption from premarket notification requirements, a final rule from the Social Security Administration on Revised Medical Criteria for Evaluating Cardiovascular Disorders, and a proposed rule from the Treasury Department's Internal Revenue Service regarding private schools.

CMS (MEDICAID/CHIP/MEDICARE)
----------------------------
A notice from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, published 2026-09-17, acknowledges the approval of an application from DNV Healthcare USA Inc. (DNV) for continued CMS recognition as a national accrediting organization for hospitals participating in the Medicare or Medicaid programs.

Another notice from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, published 2026-09-16, announces the second biannual Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) Level II public meeting of 2026. This meeting will discuss CMS preliminary coding, Medicare benefit category, and Medicare payment determinations, if applicable, for new revisions to the HCPCS Level II code set for non-drug and non-biological items and services.

Additionally, a notice from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, published 2026-09-16, announces the annual adjustment to the amount in controversy (AIC) threshold amounts for the Medicare appeals process. These adjusted amounts are effective for requests for ALJ hearings and judicial review filed on or after January 1, 2027, with the calendar year 2027 amounts set at $200 for ALJ hearings and $2,000 for judicial review.

SNAP
----
A series of notices were published by the Agriculture Department, Food and Nutrition Administration, on 2026-09-15. These notices concern Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program demonstration projects for several states.

The notices invite the public to comment on demonstration projects for North Dakota, Virginia, Nevada, Wyoming, Missouri, Texas, Arkansas, Florida, Hawaii, Idaho, Kansas, Oklahoma, Montana, Utah, Indiana, Louisiana, South Carolina, and Ohio.

Each notice specifies that the demonstration project is authorized under section 17(b) of the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008, 7 U.S.C. 2026(b). The Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA) stated in each notice that it will take appropriate action as necessary in response to comments.

TANF
----
An information collection request from the Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, published 2026-09-17, concerns the Diaper Distribution Demonstration and Research Pilot. The Office of Community Services (OCS), Administration for Children and Families (ACF), is proposing to continue collecting data on diaper need and outcomes for beneficiaries of the Diaper Distribution Demonstration and Research Pilot (DDDRP) under OMB #: 0970-0642. This collection includes the Beneficiary Report and Beneficiary Survey, and OCS is proposing revisions to the Beneficiary Survey and adding a new Organizational Survey.

Additionally, an information collection request from the Health and Human Services Department, Children and Families Administration, published 2026-09-15, addresses the Multistate Financial Institution Data Match (MSFIDM) with Federally Assisted State Transmitted (FAST) Levy under OMB #: 0970-0196. The Office of Child Support Enforcement (OCSE), Administration for Children and Families (ACF), is requesting a 3-year extension for this match, which is set to expire on January 31, 2027. OCSE revised the MSFIDM handbook and the FAST Levy record specifications, and burden estimates were reduced by 32.71 percent.

CROSS-PROGRAM
-------------
A final rule from the Agriculture Department's Agricultural Marketing Service, published on 2026-09-18, amends the pricing provisions in the 11 Federal Milk Marketing Orders (FMMOs). This rule corrects the Class I differential in Morgan County, Ohio, and removes Shannon County, South Dakota, from the Class I Differential table.

The Food and Drug Administration, acting under the Health and Human Services Department, published a final rule on 2026-09-17 concerning a partial exemption from premarket notification requirements for certain radiology computer-aided detection and/or diagnosis devices and computer-aided triage and notification devices. This order follows the FDA's denial of a petition regarding these devices.

Additionally, the Social Security Administration published a final rule on 2026-09-16 titled Revised Medical Criteria for Evaluating Cardiovascular Disorders. Separately, the Treasury Department's Internal Revenue Service published a proposed rule on 2026-09-16 providing notice of a public hearing regarding proposed regulations that would update existing rules concerning private schools that discriminate based on race, color, or national or ethnic origin.
