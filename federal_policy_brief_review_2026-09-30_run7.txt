EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
A final rule from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, Office of the Secretary, was published on 2026-09-29, correcting technical and typographical errors in a previous final rule concerning the Medicare Program, Hospital Inpatient Prospective Payment Systems for Acute Care Hospitals (IPPS), and the Long-Term Care Hospital Prospective Payment System and Policy Changes and Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 Rates. This rule also addresses requirements for quality programs, other policy changes, and the adoption of updated versions of certain health information technology standards. Other activity included routine paperwork for Medicare Program, Medicaid/CHIP/Medicare, and Medicare Program.

CMS (MEDICAID/CHIP/MEDICARE)
----------------------------
A final rule from the Health and Human Services Department, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, Office of the Secretary, published 2026-09-29, corrects technical and typographical errors in a previous final rule.
